After the completion of the Dicasterial Palace “the imperial postal direction” under Director Karl Waniczek, knight of the St. Gregor Order and honorary citizen of Neusatz, and other offices moved in here in the above named years with the postal accounting department, the postal direction’s treasury, and one post office (administrator: Virgil Marinovich), one, as Luzian Geier maintained. At the same time the “imperial telegraph inspector for the Banat, the Batschka, the Banat military border, and Transylvania” had its’ office here.
